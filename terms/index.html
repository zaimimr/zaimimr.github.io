Terms of use

ZHouse Calendar Source Reader is intended for a small private household pilot. It is not offered as a public calendar service.

Account access

Each member chooses whether to authorize read-only Google Calendar access. The household operator selects which authorized calendars the connector reads. A member can revoke access through their Google Account.

Shared scheduling

A marked Google source event may be shown as “Busy” in the household's shared iCloud calendar. New training and date events need household approval before they are added there. Members should review proposed times and correct their source calendars when plans change.

Questions and changes

Ask the household operator about access, corrections, or removal of private records. The privacy policy explains what data is read, stored, and shared.
